### Day 5 Visitor: An Archivist

**His Question**: "I feel I am on the edge of a breakthrough, like my ideas
will change the world. How do I find this knowledge within myself?"

*Choose one of the following spreads to guide your answer to this question!*

**Tarot Spread 1: Monkey's Paw**
*It rains, it pours.*

<img src="http://visforvali.github.io/ws297y/assets/images/monkeys_paw.jpg" alt="Monkey's Paw tarot spread from Auspex">

1. Bent finger: first wish ([Ten of Swords](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/ten-of-swords-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})
2. Bent finger: second wish ([High Priestess](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/high-priestess-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})
3. Bent finger: third wish ([The Fool](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/the-fool-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})
4. Pointer: a demise ([Eight of Wands](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/eight-of-wands-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})

**Tarot Spread 2: The Ark**
*A covenant signed in blood.*

<img src="http://visforvali.github.io/ws297y/assets/images/ark.jpg" alt="The Ark tarot spread from Auspex">

1. Ark, the secret ([Nine of Pentacles](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/nine-of-pentacles-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})
2. Protector, what they give up to protect it ([Six of Cups](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/six-of-cups-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})
3. Seeker, what they will lose to uncover it ([The Sun](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/the-sun-meaning-tarot-card-meanings){:target="_blank"})

**Post-Prediction Journaling**: What world-changing chain of events do they incite? How does their life stay mundane despite their impact?
