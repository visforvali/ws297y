the online workshop “Everyday Altars.” Inspired by queer-feminist scholar Ann Cvetkovich’s concept of the “bibliographic altar,” this event invites participants to create altars through the act of gathering and arranging objects, texts, and ephemera.
After a brief presentation explaining the meaning and potentials of everyday altars, attendees will have time to select and meaningfully assemble objects from around their homes that have informed and inspired their studies, creative directions, and lives. At the end of the session, willing participants will be invited to share and discuss their creations with the group.
