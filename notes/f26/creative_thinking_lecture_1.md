# Reclaim the World: Question Your Teaspoons

*Talking points and discussion notes to be posted after class.*


Metacognition consists of the conscious, deliberate beliefs and strategies we use to select, plan and progress towards, and complete our goals and are usually acquired via enculturation, i.e., processes and practices you're raised in that are therefore invisible and "natural" to you (a notable exception being neurodivergent folks who don't or can't always internalize these strategies and instead internalize ableism).
