## Show Up and Be Real

You might not be used to hearing this, let alone believing it, from a professor &mdash; but this course works best when we all show up as our authentic, imperfect, selves, ready to make mistakes and fail at things &mdash; when we treat this class like a space to chill and learn, not a space where you anxiously await judgment or avoid it by staying silent. 

We all have things to contribute, and we'll all get lost and trip up along the way. Nurturing your curiosity despite those errors and learning to productively, vocally engage with discomfort is what creative writing, resistance, and advocacy work are all about.
