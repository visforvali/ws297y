Dr. Meghana V. Nayak is a Professor of Political Science and Chair of the Women's and Gender Studies Department at Pace University - NYC. Her research, teaching, and advocacy focus on gender and racial violence, particularly against migrant communities. Please contact her if you have an administrative issue or an issue specific to the Dept. of Women's and Gender Studies.
